Featured guest on "Dialoguri fără frontiere" (Dialogues Without Borders), a podcast series produced by the Bureau of Relations with the Diaspora of the Republic of Moldova. The show profiles Moldovans building careers abroad — their professional journeys, the experience of working in international tech and academic environments, and the connections they keep with home.
